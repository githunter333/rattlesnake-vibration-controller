===============================================================================
2. TWO GUARDS, IN THE BASE CLASS SO BOTH LAWS GET THEM
===============================================================================
GUARD 1, divergence (_reject_diverged_frf).  Per BIN, not global: a bin whose live FRF has
moved more than _FRF_DIVERGENCE_LIMIT (10.0) from the system-ID FRF reverts to its
system-ID value for that call.  One wild line cannot condemn the array, and a bin recovers
by itself when the live estimate comes back.  10.0 against run 28's healthy since_initial
of median 0.1752, max 0.8920 -- an order of magnitude of headroom -- and it catches run 30
at its 55th call.

GUARD 2, deadlock escape (_escape_deadlock_if_starved).  Fires only when the predicted
response has sat _DEADLOCK_ESCAPE_DB (20) below specification WITH NOTHING SCHEDULED for
_DEADLOCK_ESCAPE_CALLS (10) consecutive calls, then discards the live FRF and re-solves
from the system ID.

Neither guard fixes what made the estimate diverge.  They stop the law amplifying it.

9. CONGRUENCE: FORCING 0 dB OVERALL WOULD MAKE IT WORSE
===============================================================================
Question raised: the +3.04 dB overall offset comes from balancing channel errors -- good
channels high, bad channels less bad -- so could we force zero overall?

The mechanism is confirmed.  Per-channel MEAN bias on run 20:

  as run (+3.04 overall)  ch1-6 [+2.05 +2.06 +1.64 +1.03 +0.33 +1.32]  ch7 -2.16  ch8 -2.82
  scaled -1.40 dB         ch1-6 [+0.65 +0.66 +0.24 -0.37 -1.07 -0.08]  ch7 -3.56  ch8 -4.22
  scaled -3.04 (0 dB)     ch1-6 [-0.99 -0.98 -1.40 -2.01 -2.71 -1.72]  ch7 -5.20  ch8 -5.86

Forcing 0 dB overall: rms 5.39 -> 5.97, >3dB 48.8% -> 55.6%, easy six 4.13 -> 4.21.  It
OVERSHOOTS: the achievable six go from 1.40 dB average high to 1.64 dB average low, no
better, just wrong the other way, and channels 7/8 drop another 3 dB.  The +3 dB is what
is holding 7 and 8 up, exactly as suspected, and removing it is paid for entirely by them.

THE RIGHT TARGET IS ABOUT -1.4 dB, not 0.  That centres the achievable six (mean |bias|
1.40 -> 0.51, the best any uniform scale can do) and leaves the overall level near +1.6 dB
-- the honest number for a law serving 8 channels with 6 drives.

BUT THE OFFSET IS NOT THE MAIN PROBLEM.  Perfectly centred at -1.4 dB, >3dB goes 48.8% ->
49.0%.  Flat.  The out-of-tolerance count is driven by per-line SCATTER, not by the mean
offset, and no global scale touches it.  optimal_diagonal sits at 19.1% out with a similar
rms.  That 30-point gap is where the real work is, and its cause is not yet known.

CAVEAT: all of the above is an offline uniform rescale of saved run-20 spectra.  The law is
closed loop with integral action (Ki = 0.5 in run 20), so it would find its own equilibrium
rather than reproduce a post-scale.  Direction and rough magnitude should hold; exact
numbers need a run.
