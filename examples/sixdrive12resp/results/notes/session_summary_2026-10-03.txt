WHAT IT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The modification register answers "what was changed".  This answers "what do I
have".  It is organised BY LAW rather than by modification: for each of the
seven laws, what it is, how it is configured now, what changed and briefly why,
and whether to use it.  Nothing in it is new -- every figure is carried from
work already recorded -- but until now the final state of any given law had to
be assembled from three documents and the source.

The shape it settles into:

  CARRY FORWARD
    optimal_diagonal          best accuracy, 5.62 dB linear and 5.58 on the
                              moving plant, and no level loop at all
    optimal_diagonal_fast     same accuracy, and with the demotion gate
                              repaired the first reproducible drive in the
                              programme -- 17.0 / 18.4 / 16.2 V^2 across three
                              repeats against 153 / 485 / 245 before
    match_trace_pseudoinverse weakest on accuracy for a structural reason, and
                              the only one that reads the measured response and
                              holds level

  PROMISING, NOT READY
    diagonal_congruence       best raw number in the set, 5.23 dB, and has
                              never held level on any run

  BASELINES
    buzz, pseudoinverse       open loop by construction.  What "no control law"
                              looks like

  NOT RUNNABLE
    octave_band_switching     parameter collision with the base class, five-
                              line fix outstanding

Three facts are true of every law and were given their own section, because
each is the kind of thing a reader otherwise has to rediscover: the startup
cap is the only behaviour change on by default; the drive-coherence cap default
splits 1.0-off / 0.95-on between the two families; and the plant is rank 5 with
eight control channels, so two must be conceded whatever the law does.

The response-error trim got its own closing section rather than a line in a
table.  "We built this, it does not work, and here is why structurally" is the
most useful paragraph in the file for anyone deciding what to try next.
