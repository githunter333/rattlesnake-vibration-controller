  run 33   [-2.08 -2.86 -2.57 -2.32 -0.86 -0.96]  ch7 -6.81  ch8 -7.48

No prediction was registered for this run, deliberately, because nobody had looked at why
run 01 scored badly.  The answer: eight days and three sets of changes to control_laws.py
later, the law does the same thing to within a tenth of a dB.

THE 8.22 rms WAS NEVER A STALE-CODE ARTIFACT.  match_trace_pseudoinverse is simply weak on
this plant, and now we know it rather than suspect it.

AND THE NUMBERS SAY WHY, matching the law's own docstring.  Every channel sits BELOW spec
in mean bias -- all six achievable ones between -0.86 and -2.86 -- yet the overall level is
+0.17 dB.  That is only possible if a minority of lines sit far above spec while most sit
below, which is what 58.5% outside +/-3 dB at an 8.15 rms describes.  The law computes ONE
REAL SCALAR per frequency line, spec_trace/response_trace, and multiplies the whole drive
CPSD by it.  The trace is therefore correct BY CONSTRUCTION and the distribution across the
eight channels is whatever the pseudoinverse happens to give.  Nothing in the law controls
it.  optimal_diagonal, which solves per channel, lands at 19.4% out at a similar level.

5. THE PATTERN THAT IS NOW ESTABLISHED ACROSS LAWS: DRIVE DOES NOT REPEAT
===============================================================================
Run 33's accuracy reproduced run 01 to a tenth of a dB.  Its DRIVE did not:
108.12 -> 168.54 V^2, max 7.24 -> 7.88 V, a 56% increase for the same response.

That is the third law to show it:
  optimal_diagonal_fast, update off   153 -> 485 -> 245 V^2   (runs 24, 26, 27)
  match_trace_pseudoinverse           108 -> 169 V^2          (runs 01, 33)
  optimal_diagonal                     67.30 -> 67.42 -> 67.15 V^2  -- DOES repeat
  optimal_diagonal_fast, update ON     17.0 -> 18.4 -> 16.2 V^2     -- DOES repeat

The two that repeat are the two that are not sitting at a conditioning limit: the
coherence-capped SDP, and the restricted factored solve with drive_rcond.  The two that do
not are the unconstrained factored solve and a pseudoinverse at rcond = 1e-3.  Hypothesis,
not finding -- match_trace has been run twice, which is enough to notice and not enough to
conclude.

CAVEAT THIS PUTS ON YESTERDAY'S CONGRUENCE ANALYSIS.  The 09-19 log computes the optimal
uniform rescale for congruence, and concludes that forcing 0 dB overall would make every
measure worse and that about -1.4 dB is the right target.  All of that came from ONE run's
spectra.  If congruence's +3.04 dB offset moves run to run the way drive does, that -1.4 dB
is one sample rather than a property of the law.  A single repeat of profile_07 settles it,
and it should happen BEFORE anyone acts on the offset analysis.
