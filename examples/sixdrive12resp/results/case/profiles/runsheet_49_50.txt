RUNS 49 and 50 -- match_trace on the BOUNDED-SOFTENING nonlinear plant
================================================================================
Fifth attempt at this experiment.  Runs 42, 44, 46 and 48 all failed.  42 for
lack of effect (single-mode article, q_rms/q0_ref = 0.0067); 44, 46 and 48 by
ESCAPE -- overflow in k3s*q**3 as the softening cubic's restoring force went
negative past q_esc = wn/sqrt(|k3|).

This article cannot escape.  Not "has more margin" -- cannot.  The restoring
force is bounded below by alpha*wn^2*q and never changes sign, so the potential
well is infinitely deep and there is no runaway state to reach.  Verified by
direct time integration at strength 400 and +12 dB: finite, with the shift
saturating at 40.84% = 1 - sqrt(0.35), exactly the alpha asymptote.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THE TARGET IS 1% AND NOT MORE -- the constraint is no longer escape
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
First build targeted 5%.  Time integration measured 29%.  Two feedbacks were
missing from the calibration, both amplifying:

  1. Softening lowers modal stiffness -> response grows -> softens further.
     Mild alone (sigma ~ g^-0.75, fixed point near 5.8%).
  2. The control drive is SHAPED.  match_trace notches it at each resonance to
     flatten the response, so a moving resonance slides OFF ITS OWN NOTCH into
     a much hotter part of the drive.  That is the large term, and no
     white-noise amplitude estimate can see it.

The calibration is now self-consistent -- qt is sized on the modal response AT
the softened resonance, not at the linear one.  That exposed the real limit:

    target     max loop gain     modes unstable
     0.50%         0.187              0/33
     1.00%         0.507              0/33      <-- BUILT HERE
     1.50%         0.764              0/33
     2.00%         0.996              0/33      <-- cliff
     3.00%         1.695              3/33
     5.00%         2.650              9/33      <-- jumped to 29%

Above gain 1.0 the design point is unstable: the plant does not settle there,
it JUMPS past to the far-softened stable state.  The builder now aborts if the
gain reaches 1 and warns above 0.7.

STRENGTH HAS NO CEILING ON THIS FILE.  It scales qt by 1/sqrt(strength) and
leaves alpha untouched, so the plant stays bounded at any value.  But strength
also multiplies the effective shift, and the loop-gain limit still applies --
raising strength walks toward the same 2% cliff.  USE 1.  Anything else needs
the builder re-run at that strength to re-check the gain.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRE-REGISTERED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. NO OVERFLOW, and this one is close to a certainty rather than a hope.  If
   "overflow encountered" appears at all, the fault is NOT the calibration --
   the kernel is provably bounded, so look at the integrator timestep or at a
   plant file that is not the one intended.  Check B2 in the profile first.
2. THE SIX MODES BELOW 300 Hz MUST NOT MOVE.  Machine zero offline.  Any FRF
   drift the diagnostic reports below ~290 Hz is estimator behaviour, full stop.
   This is the control group and it is the most useful single check in the run.
3. A STEP IN THE DRIFT DIAGNOSTIC AT EVERY LEVEL CHANGE, growing with level,
   median ~0.23 across -18 -> 0 dB against a 0.0070 floor.
4. THE LEVEL LOOP MUST CONVERGE, level dB within -0.5 to +0.5.  A level-only
   loop chasing a softening plant is the textbook failure mode, and the
   softening feedback here has loop gain 0.51 -- stable, but not negligible.
   If the level walks, that is a finding about the law.
5. RUN 50's REFRESH COUNT SHOULD NOT DECAY toward zero the way run 40's did on
   the linear plant (901 -> 6-18).  The plant keeps moving with level here.
6. NO ACCURACY PREDICTION.  It is entirely possible the refresh makes things
   WORSE -- control-level FRF estimates carry more distortion than the system
   ID does.  That is why 49 and 50 are a pair.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE OPEN CAVEAT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Everything above is OPEN LOOP against run 40's fixed drive.  In the closed loop
the controller re-notches every cycle.  That could reduce the slide-off effect
(the notch tracks the resonance) or deepen it (the notch is always one cycle
behind a moving resonance).  The direction is not predictable from here, which
is exactly what the 2x margin on the loop gain is for.  If the realised shift
at 0 dB comes back well above 1%, that is the closed loop amplifying the
feedback, and it is a result worth having rather than a setup error.
