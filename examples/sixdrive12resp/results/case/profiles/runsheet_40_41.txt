WHAT RUN 39 ESTABLISHED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Per-line SHAPE change of the in-band FRF, 175 cycles, match_trace's own drive:

    settled noise floor (since previous cycle)   median 0.0070, 95th 0.0339
    pure-noise expectation for since-cycle-1     sqrt(2)*0.0070 = 0.0100
    ACTUAL since-cycle-1, settled                0.0522   -- 5.2x that

and the ratio widens through the run (2.3 -> 6.0 -> 7.0 -> 8.2) as the
estimator quiets while the offset persists.  It decays from 0.135 to a stable
asymptote, so this is a STEP, not continuous drift: cycle 1 uses the badly
averaged startup FRF, the converged estimate settles about 5% away in shape,
and stays there.  match_trace freezes its drive shape at exactly that moment.

Confirmed independently, from the saved files rather than the live diagnostic:
sysid FRF vs final control FRF gives a shape change of median 0.0485, 95th
0.1713 -- against 0.0522 from the npz.  Two sources, same answer.

No level-change artifact: the largest post-settling jump in the per-cycle
metric is 0.0123 at cycle 26 against neighbours 0.0121 and 0.0112.  The
-12 -> 0 dB transition leaves no fingerprint, so the 5% offset is
startup-estimate error, not level dependence.

THE MECHANISM, and this is the part that matters:

              median sigma_k/sigma_1, in band        sigma_6    pinv keeps
    system ID [1, 0.319, 0.085, 0.032, 0.015, 0.0017]  0.00167   6 directions
    converged [1, 0.314, 0.084, 0.032, 0.015, 0.00014] 0.00014   5 directions

The system-ID estimate's sixth singular value is inflated by estimation noise
-- 20 averages.  It sits at 0.00167, just ABOVE rcond = 1e-3, so the cycle-1
pseudoinverse retains a sixth direction that is noise.  After 175 cycles of
averaging, sigma_6 collapses by a factor of twelve to 0.00014, well BELOW
rcond, and the same pinv would keep only five.

That is the origin of everything odd about this law's startup solve: drives 5
and 6 at 40-90x the power of drives 1-4, 98.6% coherent with each other and
about 115 degrees apart -- a large, nearly-cancelling pair poured into a
direction that does not exist.  It is also why match_trace commands the most
drive on the board.  Not the law's structure: a noise-inflated sigma_6, frozen
in at cycle 1 and never revisited.

PRE-REGISTERED.  Written before the run.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DRIVE SHOULD FALL, AND FALL A LOT.  This is the primary prediction and it
   is specific: refreshed lines re-solve against a converged FRF whose sigma_6
   is below rcond, so the pinv drops from six directions to five and stops
   feeding the cancelling 5/6 pair.  Swept offline on run 33's identification,
   crossing that boundary took the raw drive trace from 2.37 to 0.24 -- a
   factor of ten.  Expect scored drive_trace well below run 39's 0.168.
   Anything from 0.02 to 0.10 would be consistent.  If drive does NOT fall,
   the refresh is not reaching the lines that carry the drive and the
   threshold is wrong.

2. ACCURACY IS GENUINELY OPEN, and it could go either way.  Dropping to rank 5
   removes a control direction: fewer degrees of freedom, possibly worse rms.
   Against that, the retained direction was noise, and a shape matched to the
   real plant should help.  Runs 01/33/39 sit at rms 8.22/8.15/8.17 and 27.5%
   of lines outside +/-3 dB.  No prediction is registered.  Both outcomes are
   informative and neither is a failure of the mechanism.

3. LEVEL MUST HOLD.  level dB within -0.15 to +0.35, as runs 01/33/39.  The
   level loop is untouched by the refresh and the refreshed lines have their
   level set explicitly from the final array.  A level excursion means
   _set_predicted_response_trace is not doing its job.

4. REFRESH COUNTS.  The console reports refreshed_lines per cycle.  Expect a
   burst early -- roughly half of 901 -- then a decline to near zero as each
   refreshed line's reference moves to the current FRF.  A count that stays
   near 450 every cycle means the threshold is below the noise floor and the
   law is re-solving on scatter; stop and go to run 41.

5. NO RUNAWAY ON ILL-CONDITIONED LINES.  _cap_drive_coherence is not
   idempotent and its PSD re-projection moves the level; that is why the level
   is set from the FINAL array.  Before that fix one line (bin 134) went
   +0.33 dB at cycle 5 and +28.44 dB at cycle 6 and dragged a law sitting at
   +0.0001 dB to +2.49 dB.  That path is now guarded, but this is the first
   run to exercise it on hardware with a partial refresh.  ABORT if the level
   walks monotonically.
