RUN 17 -- optimal_diagonal_control_fast, dB objective, drive_rcond 3e-3
===============================================================================
WHY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Run 15 (drive_rcond 1e-2) improved on run 14 but the trace still looked poor,
and scoring the saved spectral data showed why. The error was not broadband --
it sat in two places, with two different causes.

  418-428 Hz, 14X+ at -29 dB.  MOSTLY REAL. sigma_min/sigma_max is 2.2e-3
      there and the reachability floor itself is -15 to -20 dB on that channel.
      A structural null. Even unrestricted the law only reaches 16.9 dB rms.

  985-1000 Hz, 13X+ at -26 dB and 8X+ at +14 dB.  SELF-INFLICTED. The floor
      says these lines are reachable to about +/-1 dB. sigma_min/sigma_max is
      8.1e-3 here -- just BELOW the 1e-2 threshold -- so drive_rcond cut the
      sixth drive direction exactly where the plant needed it. Restoring it
      takes that band from 8.08 to 3.40 dB rms.

The 1e-2 default was chosen off a sweep averaged over a decimated sample of
the whole band, which hid the fact that this plant's sixth singular direction
runs between 2e-3 and 8e-3 of sigma_max depending on frequency. A 1e-2
threshold does not separate a negligible direction from a useful one -- it
slices through the middle of the distribution and cuts the direction
everywhere.

  1e-3      2.19    3.68   4.58       47.7            3.00
  floor     2.33    4.43   3.85         --              --

Accuracy is bought very nearly linearly with drive voltage, because the only
way to reach 13X+ and 14X+ is to push hard down a direction the plant barely
responds to. 3e-3 recovers the self-inflicted band-edge failure without
chasing the last 0.2 dB at 48 V.

Run 15 MEASURED 8.6 V rms max per shaker against 9.3 V predicted, so the model
is tracking the rig closely on drive. Expect roughly 28 V rms at 3e-3.

NOTE: no voltage limits are set in the channel table (Maximum Value (V) is
empty on all six drive channels), so nothing in Rattlesnake will stop this.
On the virtual state-space plant that is fine. On real hardware anything past
about 10 V rms to the amplifier would be out of range -- this parameter would
have to be chosen against the amplifier, not against the floor.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WOULD BE THE REAL FIX
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
drive_rcond is a proxy. The quantity that actually matters is drive VOLTAGE per
channel, and a per-channel drive limit in the solve would express the real
constraint directly instead of through the plant's conditioning. That is a
larger change and is not in this run.
