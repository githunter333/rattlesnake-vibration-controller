WHY THE SINGLE-MODE PLANT FAILED, AND WHY THIS ONE SHOULD NOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Computed from the system model and run 40's actual commanded drive, with the
voltage-to-force scaling now VERIFIED rather than assumed -- the virtual
hardware builds B = [[0],[M^-1 phi_exc^T]] and feeds the drive signal straight
in as force, with transformation = identity, so 1 V = 1 N exactly:

    single mode   q_rms/q0_ref = 0.0067    (one 120 Hz mode, ref 20 V RMS)
    all modes     q_rms/q0_ref = 0.2097 worst, 0.1139 median over 33 modes
                                          (ref 2.5 V RMS)

A factor of 31 on the best mode.  Two reasons: the nonlinearity is on all 33
flexible modes, so the whole broadband drive contributes instead of one mode's
projection; and REFERENCE_DRIVE_LEVEL_VRMS was already rescaled from 20.0 to
2.5 in the builder, commented "to match observed closed-loop drive RMS
(~1.7-5.6 V across -12 dB to 0 dB)".  Someone hit this before.

Frequency shift goes as amplitude SQUARED, so using the builder's own
calibration (-4% at A = 2*q0 for the first four modes, -3% +/- 1.5% randomized
for the other 29):

    strength    1   worst mode -0.044 %   median in band -0.013 %
    strength    4   worst mode -0.176 %   median in band -0.052 %
    strength   25   worst mode -1.099 %   median in band -0.324 %

WHAT THE LEVEL STEP ITSELF DOES -- the number that decides the strength.

The experiment is not only "system ID at 1 V, control at 0 dB".  The law
CONVERGES at -12 dB and then the plant moves underneath it when the level
steps to 0 dB.  Drive amplitude scales by 0.251 and frequency shift goes as
amplitude squared, so at -12 dB the modes sit at 6.3% of their 0 dB shift.
That step is the perturbation, and unlike the startup-estimate offset it
arrives at a known moment.

Predicted per-line SHAPE change caused by the step, computed by rebuilding K
with the shifted modal frequencies and scoring it with the SAME normalised
metric the gate uses (run 39 on the linear plant: noise floor 0.0070,
startup-estimate offset 0.0522):

    strength   median mode shift   d_shape median    95th    vs noise
        1           -0.013 %           0.0017       0.0060     0.2x
        4           -0.052 %           0.0067       0.0238     1.0x
       25           -0.324 %           0.0418       0.1481     6.0x
      100           -1.297 %           0.1658       0.5476    23.7x

STRENGTH 100 IS THE STARTING POINT.  Two reasons, and the first is decisive:

  At strength 25 the median line's shape change is 0.0418, BELOW the 0.05
  refresh threshold.  The gate being tested would not fire on most of the
  band -- only the top quarter would trip.  Testing a gate with a stimulus
  that sits under its threshold is not a test.

  At 25 the step effect (0.042) is also the same size as the startup-estimate
  offset already present (0.052), so the two are confounded in magnitude.
  They are separable in TIME -- the startup offset settles by about cycle 30,
  the step arrives when the level changes -- but at strength 100 the step is
  3x the startup offset and no separation argument is needed.

At strength 100, shift/zeta = 1.30/4 = 0.32, still well below where a Duffing
oscillator jumps (which needs shift/zeta approaching 1).  Strength 25 remains
the gentler fallback if 100 misbehaves.

RUN 44 SYSTEM ID, MEASURED 2026-09-23 -- distortion inflates the spectrum
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Noticed on the bench as "significantly noisier than the linear sysid", and it
is, but the important part is not the noise.

                              run 39 LINEAR      run 44 ALL-MODES NL s=100
  multiple coherence median      0.9978                  0.9910
  coherence 5th pct              0.9715                  0.8973
  sigma_k/sigma_1          1 .319 .085 .032 .015 .00167   1 .439 .201 .083 .052 .0106
  condition sigma_1/sigma_6        597                      94

EVERY small singular value is lifted and sigma_6/sigma_1 rises by a factor of
6.3.  The plant cannot actually be six times better conditioned -- it is the
same M, C, K at low amplitude.  This is distortion power filling the weak
singular directions: nonlinear distortion is not confined to the linear
plant's column space, so it pours incoherent energy into exactly the
directions that were near-null and the SVD reads it as signal.  The coherence
drop and the sigma inflation are one phenomenon seen twice.

CONSEQUENCE FOR RANK, in band:

  absolute rcond 1e-3   linear: rank 6 on 72% of lines
                        nonlinear: rank 6 on 99% of lines
  gap, protect 0.05     linear: rank 5 on 76%
                        nonlinear: rank 5 on 66%

The absolute criterion has stopped discriminating altogether.  Raw startup
drive trace, median: 0.92 at rcond +1e-3 against 0.033 at rcond -0.05, a
factor of 27.

DECISION, TAKEN DELIBERATELY: run 44 and 45 go ahead at rcond = 1e-3.

  Reasons: the 44/45 pair is internally consistent whatever rcond is used, so
  the refresh comparison stays valid; it keeps comparability with runs 39/40
  on the linear plant; and it is the honest "what happens with today's
  defaults on a distorting article" case.

  Accepted cost: the startup shape is built on a sixth direction that is
  mostly distortion, so expect high drive and a possibly struggling level
  loop.  The -9 dB cap bounds only the FIRST command; steady-state drive is
  whatever that inefficient shape needs to hit the response trace.

  There is a real possibility the refresh makes things WORSE here, because
  control-level FRF estimates carry more distortion than the system ID did
  (distortion grows with drive).  That would be a finding, not a failure, and
  it is the reason to take 44 and 45 as a pair rather than 45 alone.

  RUN 46 is the follow-up: rcond = -0.05 with run 45 as its control, changing
  the rank criterion alone.  That is the gap-rank rig test that was already
  outstanding, and this article makes it far more pointed than the linear one
  would have.

Note also: the system ID runs at 1 V RMS, where q_rms/q0 is about 0.008 even
at strength 100, so the frequency shift there is about -0.007%.  The degraded
system ID is distortion, NOT the FRF having moved.  The FRF moving is what the
level step is for.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE DECISION RULE -- read run 44 before starting run 45
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The reference is run 39, the same law and diagnostics on the LINEAR plant:
    settled noise floor (since previous cycle)  0.0070
    d_ref settled (since cycle 1)               0.0522

At strength 100 the prediction is specific: a STEP in the drift diagnostic at
the moment the level changes, of median size ~0.166 and 95th ~0.55, against a
noise floor of 0.0070.  That is 24x the noise and it should be unmistakable in
the per-cycle console line.

    a clear step at the level change, d_prev jumping to ~0.1-0.2 for a few
    cycles and d_ref settling well above 0.10
        -> the plant is moving as designed.  Proceed to run 45.
    no step, d_ref settling near 0.05 like the linear plant
        -> the nonlinearity is not engaging despite the prediction, and the
           prediction is wrong somewhere.  Do NOT simply raise strength again;
           work out why first.  Do NOT read a null result from 45 as "the
           refresh does not help" when the plant never moved -- that is the
           mistake run 42 was stopped to avoid.
    the level loop fails to settle after the step
        -> a finding about the law on a moving plant.  Record it, drop to
           strength 25, and repeat.

PRE-REGISTERED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
No accuracy prediction is registered.  Nothing is known about how this law
behaves on a moving plant, and on this campaign's record my predictions about
match_trace's drive have been wrong twice.  What is registered:

1. A STEP IN THE DRIFT DIAGNOSTIC AT THE LEVEL CHANGE, median ~0.166 and
   95th ~0.55 against a 0.0070 noise floor.  This is a quantitative
   prediction from the system model, not a hope, and it gates everything
   after it.  Run 39 on the linear plant showed NO level-change artifact --
   largest post-settling jump 0.0123 against neighbours 0.0121 and 0.0112 --
   so any step here is the nonlinearity and nothing else.
2. THE LEVEL LOOP MUST CONVERGE in both.  level dB within -0.5 to +0.5.  A
   softening plant under a level-only loop is the textbook way to chase a
   moving resonance.  If the level walks, that is a finding about the law on
   a nonlinear article, and it should be recorded rather than tuned away.
3. RUN 45's REFRESH COUNT SHOULD NOT DECAY TO NEAR ZERO the way run 40's did
   (901 -> 6-18).  On a genuinely moving plant the gate should keep firing at
   a rate that tracks the level ramp.  Decay to zero again points at
   insufficient strength.
4. DRIVE IS NOT PREDICTED.  On the linear plant the refresh raised drive 20.6%
   while improving rms 9.8%; the refreshed shape uses all six drives instead
   of two nearly cancelling ones, buying accuracy by paying drive.  Whether
   that trade holds on a moving plant is unknown.
