WHY THIS RUN EXISTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Run 29 measured drive 17.0 V^2, max 2.06 V.  The offline prediction for this
law is 16.2 V^2, max 2.07 V.  That is agreement to 5%, and it would mean the
drive-realization gap -- the thing that made run 22 land at +14.29 dB and 17.8
V -- has closed once the FRF tracks the plant as driven.

It is a single run, and the standing finding from runs 24/26/27 is precisely
that accuracy repeats and DRIVE DOES NOT:

    run 24   153.4 V^2      max  6.50 V
    run 26   484.7 V^2      max 12.80 V
    run 27   245.4 V^2      max  9.83 V
    run 29    17.0 V^2      max  2.06 V     <- the claim under test

So 17.0 is exactly the kind of number that finding says to distrust.  Two
things argue it is real rather than a lucky draw: it sits a factor of 9 below
the BOTTOM of the observed spread rather than inside it, and it lands on an
independent offline prediction to 5%.  Neither is proof.  One repeat settles
it.

WHAT WOULD FALSIFY IT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Drive anywhere in the 150-500 V^2 band.  That would mean run 29 was a draw
from the same unreproducible distribution as 26 and 27, the FRF update did
not close the realization gap, and the headline claim comes out of the
findings document.

A result between about 20 and 60 V^2 is still a large improvement and still
worth reporting -- but it would have to be reported as a range, not as
"matches prediction".

EXPECTED, if run 29 was real (accuracy repeats tightly, so these should be
close):  rms about 6.0-6.1 dB, 22-23% of lines outside +/-3 dB, level within
+/-0.5 dB, easy six about 2.9, channels 7/8 about 11.7/10.3.
