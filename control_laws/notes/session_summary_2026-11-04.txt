3  THE NEW LAW FIXES THE KAPPA PROBLEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The 2026-11-03 finding: the old law's automatic kappa came out at 18 because the knee sweep
predicted each kappa with a plain fit while the real solve refits over PSD drives.  The
2026-11-04 version chooses kappa band by band and scores each candidate with the drive the
law outputs, PSD re-fit included.  Default kappa_rule 'front' (bands chosen together, one
energy price for all, knee of the error/energy front); also 'knee' and 'lcurve'.  Same
interface and attribute names, so it drops into the 10x14 harness.

tests/bandavg_versions.py runs both versions, 10x14, 15 cycles, 20-frame estimates, df 5,
oct_order 6, drive RMS limit off.  rms dB error in the law's own bands, all 14 channels / the
11 the SDP law reaches:
                                    bands, own def.   per line        drive    first solve
    OLD  auto kappa (18)            4.57 / 3.36       5.12 / 3.27     -26.5    0.07 s
    OLD  condition_number 100       2.81 / 0.94       4.43 / 2.29     -21.7    0.03 s
    NEW  front (default)            2.84 / 0.73       4.52 / 2.18     -22.9    2.1 s
    NEW  knee                       2.81 / 0.59       4.49 / 2.26     -21.8    2.1 s
    NEW  lcurve                     2.80 / 0.52       4.68 / 2.39     -19.2    2.1 s
    NEW  condition_number 100       2.81 / 0.94       4.43 / 2.29     -21.7    0.04 s
    (SDP law, same definition: 2.92 / 0.11, drive about -12 dB)
* With a fixed kappa the two versions agree to every digit, so they differ only in how kappa
  is chosen.
* 'front' picks a median kappa of 100 (range 24-562 over 21 bands); 'lcurve' reaches up to
  100000 and spends more drive.
* The sweep costs about 2 s once, at the first solve, not in the control loop.
* One plant, one run; the drive limit is off.
DECISION: do not implement the planned "knee from the real solve" option in this repo's
band_average.py; it exists upstream.  Plots of the new law: see below.

10x and 8x 3-4 dB; elsewhere the control sits on the target.  On 7x, 13x and 14x it follows
the best-achievable curve closely.
